Carbon-Composite Steering Column Mount

Image / Rendering Placeholder

Replace `images/steering-column-cad.jpg` with your image path or video iframe

Overview

Redesigned the steering column mount in Siemens NX as a high-performance carbon fibre composite assembly to replace a heavier aluminum bracket on the Formula Student vehicle.

Technical Objectives

  • Maintain structural stiffness and strength under peak steering column bending and torsional loads.
  • Ensure distributed and reliable load transfer at the chassis mounting points.
  • Meet strict Formula Student regulation constraints and composite manufacturing limitations.

Key Metrics & Impact

Weight Reduction 58.6% Drop
Mass Saved 295g to 122g
Competition Testing 100% Load Success

Methodology

Selected a carbon fibre prepreg with a Rohacell sandwich core structure. Fiber orientations were tailored specifically to align with primary bending moments and steering reaction torques.

Designed a 3-part epoxy tooling board mold to guarantee clean release after oven curing. Worked directly with Penta Patterns to create production drawings with GD&T applied to ensure tight mounting alignment tolerances.

Tools & Framework

Siemens NX CAD Prepreg Carbon Fibre Rohacell Core Sandwich Epoxy Tooling Mold Penta Patterns GD&T

Learning Outcome

Built on knowledge of composite ply orientation strategies and sandwich core boundary integration, gaining direct insight into manufacturing constraints for oven-cured prepreg structures. Learned the importance of GD&T when conveying key manufacturing information to external manufacturers.

Formula Student EV Rear Suspension

Image / Rendering Placeholder

Replace `images/rear-suspension-fea.jpg` with your image path or video iframe

Overview

Redesigned the suspension members for the Formula Student EV platform including wishbones, push/pull-rods and track-rods. Legacy carbon tubes with 3D printed titanium inserts proved vulnerable to fatigue and bonding failures during track testing.

Technical Objectives

  • Accommodate higher bump load cases resulting from increased vehicle mass.
  • Achieve higher structural safety factors while restricting overall weight penalty.
  • Ensure design can be fabricated completely in-house to respect team budget constraints.

Key Metrics & Impact

Safety Factor 2.6 Peak Load
Structural Material T45 / E45 Steel
Deflection Control Low deflection under peak load

Methodology

Evaluated suspension load cases to map primary force paths. Selected T45 seamless steel tubing combined with E45 welded steel inserts to ensure reliable joint fatigue performance.

Optimized tube outer diameters and wall thicknesses in Siemens Nastran FEA. Set up simulations for each loadcase scenario and validated weld geometries and stress concentrations under combined braking, cornering, and acceleration conditions.

Tools & Framework

NX Nastran Siemens NX Hand Calculations Load Path Analysis Deflection Study

Learning Outcome

Gained a practical understanding of transient effects on structural joints, reinforcing the importance of metallic weld integrity over bonded composite alternatives under high cyclic fatigue.

Laser-Cut Steel Wishbone Jig

Image / Rendering Placeholder

Replace `images/wishbone-jig.jpg` with your image path or video iframe

Overview

Designed an interlocking laser-cut steel welding jig in Siemens NX in collaboration with industry sponsor 'Metlase'. Replaced legacy wood/MDF fixtures to ensure repeatable dimensional accuracy when tacking and welding suspension wishbones.

Technical Objectives

  • Guarantee tight geometric tolerances for wishbone pickup hardpoints during welding.
  • Provide comfortable torch access for welders to tack-weld small steel components before final seams.
  • Eliminate fixture warping caused by thermal expansion during welding processes.

Key Metrics & Impact

Laser Accuracy 20µm Precision
Assembly Fit Seamless Lock
Sponsor Technology Metlase Slot-Rivet

Methodology

Utilized Siemens NX Tab functions alongside Metlase's slot-rivet mechanical fastening system. Structured the CAD assembly hierarchy to allow rapid modification for different suspension geometry iterations.

Exported tab geometry as DXF vector profiles directly into laser cutting software. Physical sheet steel components self-aligned seamlessly without manual positioning jigs.

Tools & Framework

Siemens NX CAD Metlase Slot-Rivet DXF Export 20µm Laser Cutting

Learning Outcome

Learned how design-for-manufacture (DFM) principles and industrial slot-rivet joining techniques drastically reduce assembly time and eliminate fixture tolerances in precision motorsport tooling.

1970's Planetary Mixer Restoration

Image / Photo / Video Placeholder

Replace `images/mixer-restoration.jpg` with your image path or video iframe

Overview

An interest-driven electro-mechanical teardown and refurbishment of a non-functional 1970s commercial planetary mixer. Tackled limited online documentation by systematically reverse-engineering internal gear engagement systems.

Technical Objectives

  • Diagnose cause of gear transmission failure across 2 out of 3 gear settings.
  • Overhaul motor assembly, internal gear drive shaft, and oil sealing interfaces.
  • Restore unit to fully functional daily use for sourdough dough processing.

Key Metrics & Impact

Asset Valuation £50 to £500
Gears Restored 3/3 Functional
Primary Fault Diving Key Spring

Methodology

Disassembled the entire gear housing and motor, logging all component sequences. Identified a broken diving key spring in the primary transmission shaft as the root failure cause.

Sourced replacement hardware from Liverpool, serviced the planetary gearbox with fresh high-pressure grease, fitted new oil seals, and re-sprayed the exterior bodywork.

Tools & Framework

Mechanical Diagnosis Gearbox Servicing Oil Seal Swap Chassis Paint Refinishing

Learning Outcome

Strengthened reverse-engineering intuition and mechanical troubleshooting methodology by diagnosing complex planetary gear trains without service manuals.

2-Stage e-Bike Gearbox & FEA Validation

Image / FEA Render / Video Placeholder

Replace `images/acrylic-gearbox-fea.jpg` with your image path or video iframe

Overview

University engineering project focused on designing a 2-stage acrylic speed reducer. The gearbox steps down high-RPM power from a 250W motor to drive a bicycle wheel while complying with UK legal e-bike top speed limits (15 mph).

Technical Objectives

  • Maximize energy efficiency and travel distance within strict material constraints (2 sheets of acrylic).
  • Ensure gear teeth and spline drive attachments survive maximum motor startup torque.
  • Maintain minimum Safety Factor of 2.0 under continuous operating loads.

Key Metrics & Impact

FEA Load Case 4x 59N Bearing Load
Speed Limit 15 mph Compliant
Prototype Result Passed 1st Try

Methodology

Determined exact acrylic material properties using a Shimadzu material testing machine. Applied gear kinematics and beam stress equations to size straight-cut spur gears.

Validated spline attachments and gear tooth bending stress in Fusion 360 FEA (4x 59N bearing loads constrained at fin edges). Manufactured and bench-tested the assembly, hitting all distance and torque performance metrics without material yield.

Tools & Framework

Shimadzu Tensile Machine Fusion 360 FEA Acrylic Fabrication Gear Kinematics

Learning Outcome

Learned how empirical material testing data (via Shimadzu machines) directly improves the predictive accuracy of Fusion 360 FEA simulations when working with brittle engineering polymers.
